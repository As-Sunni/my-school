About us

## A school where faith and learning grow together

Sa'adah Islamic Academy brings together a strong academic curriculum and a firm Islamic upbringing. Every child is guided to know their Lord, love their deen, respect their parents and teachers, and pursue excellence in every field of knowledge.

Our teachers work closely with parents so that what is taught in the classroom is lived at home, and progress is shared openly through our online parent portal.

“Whoever travels a path seeking knowledge, Allah makes easy for him a path to Paradise.”

Hadith, Sahih Muslim

Programmes

## From first steps to graduation

🧸

### Early Years

Play-based learning, early Arabic letters, short surahs, duas and good manners in a warm, safe setting.

📘

### Primary School

Literacy, numeracy and science alongside Qur'an recitation, Islamic studies and Arabic.

🔬

### Junior Secondary

A broad curriculum that builds study skills, critical thinking and leadership with strong Islamic values.

🎓

### Senior Secondary

Focused subject tracks and exam preparation, with mentoring for university and career choices.

📖

### Qur'an & Tajweed

Daily recitation, memorisation and tajweed classes led by qualified teachers.

🕌

### Islamic Studies

Aqeedah, fiqh, seerah, hadith and Arabic language taught step by step for every age group.
